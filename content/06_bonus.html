A redacted audio clip of the 22-minute call. I pause it every 2-3 minutes and explain what I was thinking, what they were signaling, and where I anchored the number.

68:30

Two rounds of revisions, what I pushed back on, what I conceded. How the final invoice was structured. The exact day payment cleared.

80:00

What I'd change if I ran this deal again today. The two places I left money on the table. The one thing I did that I want to keep doing forever.
