// tool_03 · content_prompts

    
30 content angles that book deals.

    
These are the angles brands pay for. Create spec content using these prompts and your portfolio sells itself.


    

      
      

        
type: performance

        
"I tried this for 30 days."

        
Time-boxed testimonial. High trust signal. Works in any niche.

      

      

        
type: performance

        
"The [adjective] thing I've added to my routine."

        
Fill in: cheapest, sneakiest, most underrated, most annoying-to-explain.

      

      

        
type: performance

        
"Before I found this, I was [problem]."

        
Classic problem/solution structure. Lead with the pain, earn the product.

      

      

        
type: social_proof

        
"My [partner/friend/mom] asked what I changed."

        
Third-party validation without paid spokespeople. High relatability.

      

      

        
type: social_proof

        
"I've been quietly recommending this for months."

        
Feels like a secret tip from a trusted friend. High share rate.

      

      

        
type: pov

        
"POV: you finally found the [product type] that works."

        
TikTok native format. Puts the viewer in the experience immediately.

      

      

        
type: pov

        
"POV: you're running late but your [product] gets you there."

        
Urgency + product benefit in one scene. Great for beauty, fashion, food.

      

      

        
type: educational

        
"Why [common belief] is wrong."

        
Contrarian hook. Stops scrollers. Works for supplements, skincare, fitness.

      

      

        
type: educational

        
"The [number] things I wish I knew about [category]."

        
List format. Easy to watch to the end. Strong completion rate.

      

      

        
type: unboxing

        
"I finally caved and ordered it — first impressions."

        
Authentic unboxing energy. Works even if you're familiar with the brand.

      

      

        
type: unboxing

        
"Trying every product in the [brand] haul."

        
Multi-product format. Great for brands with wide SKU range.

      

      

        
type: grwm

        
"My get ready with me ft. [brand]."

        
Natural product integration. Works best when the product genuinely fits the routine.

      

      

        
type: comparison

        
"I tried [expensive brand] vs. [brand] — here's what happened."

        
Comparison content performs well. Anchor to a well-known competitor.

      

      

        
type: routine

        
"My entire morning routine with [brand] products."

        
Full integration. Feels authentic when it's an honest reflection of real use.

      

      

        
type: bold_claim

        
"This is the only [product type] I'll ever use."

        
Strong conviction hook. Only use when you actually mean it — delivers on authenticity.
