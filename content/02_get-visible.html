// tool_03 · content_prompts

    
30 content angles that book deals.

    
These are the angles brands actually pay for. Create spec content using these prompts and your portfolio sells itself.


    

      

        
type: performance

        
"I tried this for 30 days."

        
Time-boxed testimonial. High trust. Works in every niche — the timeline makes the result feel credible.

      

      

        
type: performance

        
"Before I found this, I was [problem]."

        
Classic problem/solution. Lead with the pain. Earn the product. Never start with the product.

      

      

        
type: performance

        
"The [adjective] thing I've added to my routine."

        
Fill in: cheapest, sneakiest, most underrated, most annoying-to-explain. Specificity = clicks.

      

      

        
type: confessional

        
"This is what my brain sounds like at 3am when [problem]."

        
Private therapy energy. Raw, internal monologue. Viewer feels seen before the product even shows up.

      

      

        
type: confessional

        
"I've been quietly using this and pretending my [result] is just genetics."

        
Secret-keeper energy. Feels like a real confession. High share rate — people tag their friends.

      

      

        
type: confessional

        
"I asked ChatGPT why nothing was working for my [problem]. It pointed me straight to [product type]."

        
AI comparison hook — feels timely, curiosity-first. Leads with a relatable frustration, not a pitch.

      

      

        
type: pov

        
"POV: it's 3am and you're down a rabbit hole about why this product is everywhere."

        
TikTok native. Pulls the viewer into the moment before they even realize they're watching an ad.

      

      

        
type: pov

        
"POV: your friend asks what you changed and you have to tell them it was a $[price] [product]."

        
Social proof baked into the hook. Third-party validation without paid influencers.

      

      

        
type: pov

        
"POV: you finally stop settling for the version that kind of works."

        
Identity shift hook. Positions the viewer as someone who's done accepting less. Strong for premium products.

      

      

        
type: bold_claim

        
"Hot take: this is better than the [expensive brand] I was using."

        
Anchor comparison. Signals you've tested alternatives. Stops scrollers who know the brand you're comparing to.

      

      

        
type: bold_claim

        
"Okay I've been keeping this quiet for months because I wasn't ready to share."

        
Scarcity + personal investment. Makes the product feel discovered, not marketed.

      

      

        
type: bold_claim

        
"I don't care what anyone says — this is worth every penny."

        
Conviction hook. Signals you've heard the objections and you're still recommending it. Disarms price sensitivity.

      

      

        
type: question

        
"Why is nobody talking about the fact that this works better than [alternative]?"

        
Contrarian + curiosity. Gets comments from both believers and skeptics — both boost reach.

      

      

        
type: question

        
"Why did nobody tell me about [product] before I spent [amount] on things that didn't work?"

        
Relatable frustration. Speaks to everyone who's been in the cycle of trying and failing.

      

      

        
type: story

        
"I almost returned this. Then I gave it one more week. Big mistake — the good kind."

        
Tension arc. Viewers stay to find out what changed. High completion rate from second 3 onward.

      

      

        
type: story

        
"My [friend/dermatologist/trainer] told me to try this. I rolled my eyes. I was wrong."

        
Credibility transfer + humility. Feels authentic. Works especially well with skeptical audiences.

      

      

        
type: transformation

        
"[Timeframe] of using this. Here's what actually changed."

        
Result-first framing. The timeline creates credibility. Always be specific: "6 weeks" beats "a while."

      

      

        
type: transformation

        
"The only thing that changed in my routine was this [product]. So."

        
Confident, understated. The "So." at the end does a lot. Confidence = credibility in UGC.

      

      

        
type: educational

        
"Why [common belief about your niche] is actually wrong."

        
Contrarian educational hook. Stops scrollers cold. Works best when the belief is widely held and you have receipts.

      

      

        
type: social_proof

        
"My [partner/roommate/mom] noticed before I said anything."

        
Unsolicited third-party validation. The fact that they noticed without being told is the proof.

      

      

        
type: unboxing

        
"I finally caved and ordered it — first impressions, no filter."

        
Authentic first-contact energy. Works even if you know the brand well — the "caved" language makes it feel real.

      

      

        
type: comparison

        
"I tried [well-known brand] vs. [brand] side by side for 3 weeks."

        
Comparison format. Anchoring to a known brand gets you traffic from people already in the market.

      

      

        
type: grwm

        
"Get ready with me — ft. the thing that's been carrying my [morning / skin / energy] lately."

        
Natural product integration. Works best when the product genuinely fits the routine and you don't over-explain it.

      

      

        
type: routine

        
"My honest [morning / night / gym] routine — including the stuff that actually works."

        
"Honest" is the trigger word. Signals you're filtering out the noise, which makes your recommendation feel earned.

      

      

        
type: relatability

        
"For anyone who has tried literally everything for [problem] and is running out of patience."

        
Speaks directly to the exhausted buyer. Not a general hook — a message to the person who's been failed by the category before.

      

      

        
type: specificity

        
"I've spent $[amount] on [category] products. This is the one I'd buy again."

        
The dollar amount gives you authority. You've done the expensive research so they don't have to.

      

      

        
type: specificity

        
"[Specific result] in [specific timeframe]. Here's what I changed."

        
Numbers + timeline = instant credibility. "My skin cleared up" is weak. "3 weeks, noticeably clearer" is strong.

      

      

        
type: scarcity

        
"I've been recommending this for months but never made a video because I was worried it'd sell out."

        
Insider energy. Makes the viewer feel like they're getting access to something not everyone knows about.

      

      

        
type: format_play

        
"Rate my [routine / stack / haul] — be honest."

        
Invites interaction. People love to give opinions. The brand shows up as part of your real routine, not as a sponsored placement.

      

      

        
type: format_play

        
"Things I was embarrassed to admit I needed... until I tried them."

        
Vulnerability hook. Lowers the viewer's guard. Works especially well for products that fix problems people don't talk about.

      

    


    

      
// the_rule

      
Sell the transformation, not the product. "This serum has 15% vitamin C and hyaluronic acid" → deleted. "My skin texture changed in 2 weeks and my coworker asked what I was doing differently" → watched to the end. Always lead with the result. The product is the reason — not the story.
