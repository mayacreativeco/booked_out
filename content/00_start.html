// the_30_day_plan · beginner_route

30 days. First paid deal.

Go from "just bought this" to landing your first paid brand deal. Build your portfolio, send 50 pitches, close one. You only need one to start.

// week_01 · days 1–7 · foundation

You are now a UGC creator.

Not when you have followers. Not when a brand pays you. Now. Read this once before Day 1. You'll come back to it on the days you want to quit.

→ You don't need a following. UGC is B2B — you sell to brands, not to followers. The number on your profile is irrelevant to anyone who's about to pay you.

→ You don't need expensive equipment. Your phone is the studio. Brands cast for skin tone and energy, not 4K cameras.

→ You don't need permission to start. Filming spec content for a brand you love is allowed and encouraged. It's how every booked-out creator I know got their first deal.

→ "I make [niche] content for brands" is a complete sentence. Use it when anyone asks what you're doing now. The identity comes first. The income follows.

done_when: you've read this and you're ready to go to D1.

You can't pitch a brand if you don't know what kind of creator you are. Run the niche selector in Get Clear and write your positioning sentence before you do anything else today.

Then: tell one person what you're doing. Say it out loud — to a friend, a partner, a sibling, anyone. The accountability gap closes the second you say it out loud. You don't need their permission. You just need to hear yourself say it.

done_when: "I make [style] UGC for [niche] brands" — and you believe it. And one person knows.

Run the Portfolio Checklist. Score out of 12. Under 8? Pick the 3 highest-leverage gaps from the checklist and fix only those before D7. The rest you fix as you go. You don't need a 12 to start pitching — you need a 9. Waiting for perfect is waiting for nothing.

done_when: score noted. if under 8, next 3 days = fix the gaps.

Pick a brand you genuinely love — something you already own or use. Go to Get Visible → Script Builder, build a 30-second script, and film it. A real video shot today beats a perfect concept that never gets filmed.

done_when: 1 piece filmed and somewhere shareable. phone camera is fine.

Use the Rate Calculator. Get your floor (the lowest you'll ever accept) and your opening quote (floor + 25%). Write both numbers somewhere physical. You need them memorized before any brand reaches out.

done_when: floor_rate and opening_quote are written. stop adjusting per-brand.

Get your Client CRM running. Pick Brkaway (recommended), Notion, or Airtable — full setup guide in 05_get-consistent. The moment you pitch someone, they go in the CRM — stage: pitched. This is how you run a real business, not a hobby.

done_when: tracker exists and is empty. it won't stay empty long.

Second piece for the same brand or a different one in your niche. Try a different format: if D3 was talking-head, try unboxing or GRWM today. Brands want to see range. Use the Content Prompts for angles that work.

done_when: 2 pieces total in portfolio. different formats if possible.

Open Offer Positioning in Get Clear. Use the 4-part formula to write one sentence that describes exactly what you do. This goes in your bio, your email signature, your media kit. Write it once, use it everywhere.

done_when: one offer sentence written. not a paragraph. one sentence.
// week_02 · days 8–14 · build the pipeline

Third piece. Now you have a portfolio. Build the actual portfolio page in Canva (easiest, most polished), Pressbook.co (purpose-built for creators), or a Notion page if you want fast and free. Store the actual video files in Google Drive or Dropbox — organized by niche, then by brand, so you can share a specific folder instantly when a brand asks.

done_when: 3 pieces, one shareable link. portfolio page + video storage = both set up.

Open Meta Ad Library and search your niche — brands actively running video ads are already buying UGC. To find the right contact: search LinkedIn ("head of social" or "brand manager" + brand name), use Instagram DMs for smaller indie brands under ~50K followers (they respond fast), or use Hunter.io to find emails directly. For brands above that size or with a serious marketing team, skip the DM — find the LinkedIn or email. DMs to bigger brands get routed to a general inbox and ignored. Add 10 to your tracker at stage: cold.

// Note: tools like Onbento, Apollo.io, and Lemlist are covered in 05_consistent for building a real email outbound system — save those for when you're ready to automate and scale.

done_when: 10 rows in tracker, stage = cold, contact found for each.

Go to 04_booked → Pitch Generator. Build your template. Remember the rule: first message = warm, short, no rates. Just: "I love your brand, I make content in this space, would you be open to chatting?" Rates come in message two, after they reply.

done_when: 1 pitch template drafted. warm, short, no rates, no portfolio link yet.

Take your template and customize it for your top 5 brands. For each: reference something specific — a recent product launch, a campaign you noticed, a piece of content they posted. One genuine, researched line per pitch. That's what makes it land instead of get deleted.

done_when: 5 customized drafts saved. one specific reference in each.

Back to Meta Ad Library. Look at adjacent niches too — beauty → skincare tools → wellness supplements. You want 50 pitches sent by day 30. Pacing: 10 new brands every couple of days gets you there without burning out.

done_when: 20 rows total in tracker.

Personalized versions for your next 5 brands. You now have 10 pitches ready to send. Batching the writing separate from the sending is intentional — cold outreach takes different mental energy than personalization work.

done_when: 10 pitch drafts ready. reviewed once. ready to send.

Before you send anything: does your portfolio link work? Are your pitches personalized (not copy-paste)? Do you know your rate without thinking? Is your tracker set up? Fix anything that's still soft before you go live. This is the last checkpoint.

done_when: portfolio link ✓ · rates memorized ✓ · pitches personalized ✓
// week_03 · days 15–21 · start sending

Send your first 5 pitches. Update your tracker: stage = pitched, last contact = today. Set a reminder for D20 to follow up on any that haven't replied. Send and move on — do not check your inbox obsessively.

done_when: 5 pitches sent. tracker updated. D20 follow-up reminders set.

While pitches 1-5 are out there, build the next wave. Research 10 more brands, add to tracker. The pipeline should always be ahead of your current conversations — you want to be choosing which brand to work with, not waiting on one.

done_when: 30 brands total in tracker.

Second batch. Same rules: personalized, warm, no rates in first message. Update tracker after each. You now have 10 active pitches in the world.

done_when: 10 total pitches sent. 10 rows at stage = pitched.

If anyone replied from pitches 1-5: reply back the same day. This is where you introduce your rates for the first time — keep the same warm energy. "So glad you're interested! Here's my portfolio and how I typically work..." Share the link here, not before.

done_when: all replies answered within same business day. rates sent on reply.

Research and customize for the next wave. By now you're getting comfortable with personalizing — you've seen what real pitching looks like in your niche.

done_when: 15 total pitches sent.

5 days, no reply: one follow-up. Short. Warm. "Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried! Still really interested in working together if the timing's right." That's it. One follow-up per pitch, ever. Move on if they don't reply again.

done_when: follow-ups sent on non-replies from D15. stage updated in tracker.

20 pitches out. You're building real data. No replies at all? Look at your subject lines and opening line — something needs adjusting. Getting replies but no conversions? The issue is in how you're presenting rates. Go back to 03_paid.

done_when: 20 pitches sent. analyze reply rate — above 10%? if not, revise pitch copy.
// week_04 · days 22–30 · close the first deal

One follow-up on every non-reply from D15-D21. After that, move them to stage: no reply in your tracker. Close the loop and move forward. You'll revisit in 30 days if you want — but for now, keep volume up.

done_when: all non-replies followed up. stage updated to no_reply.

Keep the pipeline moving. If you have a warm conversation going, great — keep it warm while also building the next wave. Never stop prospecting just because one looks promising.

done_when: 40 brands total in tracker.

If a brand has replied and is interested: don't stall. Send the Proposal Template from 05_consistent. Fill it the night before, send at 9am. Deals die in the gap between "sounds good" and "here's the paperwork."

done_when: proposal sent within 24 hours of any warm reply.

By now pitching takes 10-15 minutes per brand, not an hour. Your research is faster. Your personalization is tighter. You're building a real outbound muscle — and the results compound.

done_when: 30 total pitches sent.

New content from the last 30 days signals you're active. Film something — a new angle, a new brand, a new format. Doesn't need to be perfect. Needs to be recent. Brands notice when the most recent work is 3 months old.

done_when: 4+ pieces in portfolio. most recent from this month.

Keep sending. The math is real: at a 10-15% reply rate, 40 pitches = 4-6 conversations. Of those, 1-2 will be real interest. That's your first deal. Volume isn't spam — personalized volume is strategy.

done_when: 40 pitches sent total.

Any open conversations stalling: move them. Proposal pending? Follow up. They asked for rates and went quiet? Follow up: "Hey, happy to adjust anything or answer questions if something was unclear!" No conversation sits idle for more than 72 hours.

done_when: no conversation sitting idle for more than 72 hours.

Last 10. These are brands you've been researching since week 3 — they get your best, most dialed-in versions yet. You know your niche. You know what they need. You know your rate. Pitch with confidence.

done_when: 50 pitches sent total. tracker fully up to date.

50 pitches at a realistic reply rate means 5-8 live conversations. One or two of those should be close. If you have a proposal pending — follow up today. If your reply rate is under 5% — your pitch copy needs work, not more volume. Go back to 04_booked and revise the opener.

You did the work. The first $500 is a question of when, not if.

When you close it — email me. support@mayaherring.com. Subject line: "I closed it." Even one line. I want to know. This is the part that matters.

done_when: first_deal_closed. → then go to 03_paid and raise your floor.
