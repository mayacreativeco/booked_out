// section_00 · start_here

Your 7-day launch plan.

The fastest path from "just bought this" to "first pitch sent." Do these in order. Skip nothing in week one.

// the_plan

Seven days. One action each day.

You don't need a course to finish. You need seven decisions. Make them in order and you'll have pitched your first brand by the end of week one.

D1
Lock your niche. One sentence.
→ 01_clear

You can't pitch a brand if you don't know what kind of creator you are. Run the niche selector in Get Clear and write your niche sentence before you do anything else today.

done_when: "I make [type] UGC for [niche] brands" — and you believe it.
D2
Audit your portfolio. Score it.
→ 01_clear

Run the Portfolio Checklist. Score out of 12. If you're below 8, that's where your day goes. Fix the gaps before you pitch anything.

done_when: score ≥ 8 / 12.
D3
Run your rate. Write the number down.
→ 03_paid

Use the Rate Calculator to get your range. Then write your floor — the lowest you'll ever accept — and the opening quote you'll lead with. Don't adjust these per-brand. Set them once.

done_when: floor_rate and opening_quote are written somewhere you'll see them.
D4
Build your brand target list. Five brands.
→ 04_booked

Open the Brand Contact Database and pick 5 brands in your niche that are actively running UGC. Find the right person on LinkedIn. Add them to your Client Tracker with stage: cold.

done_when: 5 rows in your tracker, stage = cold.
D5
Write three pitches. Don't send yet.
→ 04_booked

Use the Pitch Generator for your first 3 brands. Save the drafts. Sleep on them. You'll edit in the morning with fresh eyes before you send.

done_when: 3 drafts written and saved.
D6
Send two pitches. Yes, today.
→ 04_booked

Edit yesterday's drafts, pick the 2 strongest, and send them. Not schedule — send. Update your tracker to stage: pitched. Set a D10 follow-up reminder for each.

done_when: 2 pitches sent, tracker updated, follow-ups calendared.
D7
Build your weekly routine. Block the calendar.
→ 05_consistent

Open the Weekly CEO Dashboard. Block a recurring 15-minute "Monday review" on your calendar. This is how chaos turns into a business.

done_when: recurring Monday review on calendar. You are now running a business.
