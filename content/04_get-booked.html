// scripts_01 · cold_outbound

Cold outbound scripts.

The 3 variants I use for brands that have never heard of me. Pick based on what kind of proof you can lead with.

cold_01 · the_product_lead when: you've used the product
Hi [FIRST NAME],

I'm a UGC creator in the [NICHE] space, and I've been using [PRODUCT] for the last few weeks. The [1 SPECIFIC DETAIL YOU ACTUALLY NOTICED] is unreal — and I already know the exact hook I'd build around it.

I've worked with brands like [2-3 BRANDS, OR "similar DTC brands"], and my content typically drives [ONE METRIC: engagement rate, CPA, click-through].

Would you be open to seeing a 2-concept mood-board for [BRAND]? I can have it to you inside 48 hours — no commitment, just a gut-check on whether it's a fit.

Either way, big fan of what you're building.

— [YOUR NAME]
cold_02 · the_specificity_lead when: you spotted an opportunity in their ads
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Saw your [SPECIFIC AD / LAUNCH / CAMPAIGN] running on [PLATFORM] — the [WHAT'S WORKING] angle is smart. I've been thinking about how a creator-led version would hit on TikTok, and I think there's a GRWM hook in here that would print.

I'm a UGC creator who's shot for [2-3 SIMILAR BRANDS]. Would you want me to send over 2 concept directions? No commitment — just a quick gut-check on whether it's worth building.

Can I send my portfolio over so you can see the style first?

— [YOUR NAME]
cold_03 · the_results_lead when: you have real numbers to show
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Quick pitch — my recent UGC piece for [COMPARABLE BRAND] drove [METRIC: e.g. 3.2x ROAS at $8 CPA / 2.4M organic views / 62% watch-through], and I think the same framework would land for [BRAND], specifically for [PRODUCT / LAUNCH].

I'd love to send you a 2-minute Loom breaking down the 3 concept directions I'd build. Takes 0 of your time to watch, and you'll know inside 2 minutes whether it's a fit.

Want me to send it over?

— [YOUR NAME]

// scripts_03 · follow_up

Follow-up scripts.

The 3-touch sequence for leads that went quiet. Most deals close on touch 2 or 3, not touch 1.

follow_up_01 · day_4 touch 2 · short & low-pressure

Floating this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried. No pressure — just wanted to make sure you saw the concept.

If it's a "not right now", totally fine, just let me know and I'll stop bugging you.

— [YOUR NAME]
follow_up_02 · day_10 touch 3 · add new value
Hi [FIRST NAME],

One more from me. Since I sent the original pitch, I actually shot a spec concept for [BRAND] — you can see it here: [LOOM / DRIVE LINK].

It's 40 seconds. If you love it, let's talk. If not, I'll go quiet.

follow_up_03 · day_21 · the_breakup touch 4 · final · works ~30% of the time

Since I haven't heard back, I'm going to assume the timing's not right and close the loop on my end. Totally understand.

If things shift, or if you'd rather I check back in [Q2 / next quarter / after launch], just reply "later" and I'll queue it for then.

Either way, big fan of what [BRAND] is doing. Go build cool stuff.

— [YOUR NAME]

Recovery scripts.

If this doesn't work for your timeline, let me know and we can walk through options.

Thanks for the honest feedback on Round 1. I hear you on [THE SPECIFIC POINT] — that's on me for not catching it in the brief.

Here's my plan for Round 2:
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 1]
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 2]
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 3]

I'll have revised cuts to you by [DATE]. Before I start, quick gut-check: does this direction match what you had in mind, or do you want me to send a new concept board first?

— [YOUR NAME]
recovery_03 · ghosted_mid_project when: the brand stopped replying after concepts

Haven't heard back on the concept board I sent on [DATE]. I know launches get chaotic — so a quick call:

→ If it's a hard no, reply "cancel" and we'll close it out per the SOW terms.

No wrong answer — just need a direction.

— [YOUR NAME]

// tool_03 · brand_sourcing_system

Where to find brands. How to fill your pipeline.

Two separate systems — inbound (brands come to you) and outbound (you go to them). Build both. Consistent bookings come from running both tracks at once, not just one.

system_01 · inbound brands come to you · set it up once and let it run

Inbound is where brands discover you — your portfolio, your profiles, your presence on creator marketplaces. Your job is to exist in the right places with the right positioning.

LinkedIn ↗

The highest-intent inbound channel. Post UGC content, share case studies, comment on brand posts. Social media managers and marketing leads use LinkedIn daily — your best shot at warm discovery.

Instagram ↗

Keep your portfolio highlight updated with recent work. Brands DM creators they've been watching. Tagging brands in relevant content is the easiest organic inbound trigger.

Trend.io ↗

High-quality brand campaigns posted directly by brands. Apply to briefs that fit. Competitive but deal sizes are solid. Good for building case studies fast.

Cohley ↗

Brand-side brief platform. Brands post their campaigns and you apply. Best for established creators — portfolio quality matters here more than follower count.

Aspire.io ↗

Creator marketplace used by mid-to-large DTC brands. Strong in beauty, wellness, and lifestyle. Set your profile up properly and you'll get inbound briefs without lifting a finger.

Backstage ↗

Creator directory where brands search by niche, style, and availability. Keep your profile active and rate-current. Strong for beauty, wellness, and lifestyle creators.

Kale ↗

DTC-focused UGC platform with a volume-heavy model. Brands post campaigns, you apply. Deal sizes tend to run lower than direct outbound, but volume and consistency are high. Good for building reps and testimonials early on.

Vidsy ↗

Agency-model platform that matches creators with brand briefs. Higher production quality expected. Strong for video-first creators — brands include major DTC and CPG names.

Obviously ↗

Full-service influencer platform used by enterprise brands. Strong brief quality and organized campaign management. Good for creators ready to work with larger brand budgets.

system_02 · outbound you go to brands · your most controllable pipeline lever

Outbound is how you control your own pipeline. You identify the brands, you build the list, you send the pitch. The inbound system fills gaps — outbound fills your calendar.

Pick one outbound tool and commit to it. These platforms help you build targeted brand lists, find contact emails, and run follow-up sequences so nothing falls through. Don't use all four — pick the one that fits your budget and volume.

Onbento ↗ recommended

Built specifically for creators and UGC outreach. Find brand contacts by niche, build sequences, track open rates. The most creator-native option on this list.

best for: UGC creators new to outbound

Persona.space ↗ recommended

Creator-focused CRM and outbound tool. Helps you manage your brand contacts, track conversations, and run follow-up sequences in one place.

best for: creators managing 20+ active brand relationships

promo code: MAYACREATIVECO
Apollo.io ↗

Full-featured B2B prospecting tool. Find verified emails for social media managers and marketing leads at DTC brands. More powerful than Onbento, steeper learning curve.

best for: experienced creators who know their target brand profile

Lemlist ↗

Email sequencing tool used by sales teams and freelancers. Strong personalization and deliverability features. Pair with Apollo for list-building + Lemlist for sending.

best for: creators sending 50+ personalized pitches per month

// troubleshooting · outbound_not_working

Outbound diagnostic.

reply_rate < 5% after 20 pitches

The subject line is the issue, not the body. Most pitches die before they're read. Rewrite your subject line first — test 3 different angles before changing anything else.

getting replies but no conversions

Your message-2 rate framing is the issue. Re-read 03_paid — specifically how to frame your rate on second contact. The reply rate is fine; the close rate isn't.

closing deals but they're all $150–300

Your floor in the calculator is too low. Raise it 25% and re-run. The brands finding you at $150 are finding you because you've signaled that price. Change the signal.

brands ghosting mid-conversation

Your proposal is taking too long. Send it inside 24 hours of verbal yes. Momentum dies in the gap between "we're interested" and paperwork — go to 05_consistent and pre-build your proposal now.
