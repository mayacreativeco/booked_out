// on_ai_and_ugc · 2026

Yes, AI UGC is improving fast. No, it's not replacing the creators who get booked.

Brands hire human UGC for two reasons: trust and conversion. Synthetic creators don't carry either. The brands chasing AI-only UGC are mostly the brands you don't want to work with — low budget, low results, high churn.

Your moat: your face, your specific niche knowledge, your real client roster. None of that is replaceable by a model. What's at risk: generic, niche-agnostic UGC made by creators who don't have a clear positioning. This kit is built to make sure you're not that creator.
