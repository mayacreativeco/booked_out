The exact words for the moments that cost you money.

Four script categories. Every one is battle-tested on real brand deals. Copy the variant that fits your situation, swap the placeholders, hit send.

// scripts_01 · cold_outbound

Cold outbound scripts.

The 3 variants I use for brands that have never heard of me. Pick based on what kind of proof you can lead with.

cold_01 · the_product_lead when: you've used the product
Hi [FIRST NAME],

I'm a UGC creator in the [NICHE] space, and I've been using [PRODUCT] for the last few weeks. The [1 SPECIFIC DETAIL YOU ACTUALLY NOTICED] is unreal — and I already know the exact hook I'd build around it.

I've worked with brands like [2-3 BRANDS, OR "similar DTC brands"], and my content typically drives [ONE METRIC: engagement rate, CPA, click-through].

Would you be open to seeing a 2-concept mood-board for [BRAND]? I can have it to you inside 48 hours. Starting rate is $[RATE] for 3 × 30-second verticals with 30 days paid usage.

Either way, big fan of what you're building.

— [YOUR NAME]
cold_02 · the_specificity_lead when: you spotted an opportunity in their ads
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Saw your [SPECIFIC AD / LAUNCH / CAMPAIGN] running on [PLATFORM] — the [WHAT'S WORKING] angle is smart. I've been thinking about how a creator-led version would hit on TikTok, and I think there's a GRWM hook in here that would print.

I'm a UGC creator who's shot for [2-3 SIMILAR BRANDS]. Would you want me to send over 2 concept directions for free? No commitment — just a quick gut-check on whether it's worth building.

If you like what you see, my package starts at $[RATE] for a 3-video drop with 30 days paid usage.

— [YOUR NAME]
cold_03 · the_results_lead when: you have real numbers to show
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Quick pitch — my recent UGC piece for [COMPARABLE BRAND] drove [METRIC: e.g. 3.2x ROAS at $8 CPA / 2.4M organic views / 62% watch-through], and I think the same framework would land for [BRAND], specifically for [PRODUCT / LAUNCH].

I'd love to send you a 2-minute Loom breaking down the 3 concept directions I'd build. Takes 0 of your time to watch, and you'll know inside 2 minutes whether it's a fit.

If it lands, package starts at $[RATE].

— [YOUR NAME]

// scripts_02 · warm_followup

Warm re-engagement scripts.

For brands that have worked with you before or already know who you are. The goal: open the door to a second project.

warm_01 · past_client_reactivation when: 60+ days since the last project
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Circling back because I've been sitting on a concept I think would be perfect for [BRAND] — especially after how well the [PREVIOUS PROJECT] performed.

Idea in one line: [YOUR ONE-LINE CONCEPT].

I'm holding capacity for one more project in [MONTH] and wanted to offer you first look. Same package structure as last time ($[RATE]), same turnaround.

Want me to send a full brief?

— [YOUR NAME]
warm_02 · usage_expiring when: their paid-usage window is about to close
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Heads up — the usage window on the [PROJECT NAME] content expires on [DATE]. If those pieces are still performing for you, we have a few options:

→ Extend usage for another 90 days at $[EXTENSION RATE]
→ Extend for 12 months at $[ANNUAL RATE]
→ Refresh the set with 2 new videos + roll prior usage for another 30 days at $[REFRESH RATE]

Happy to run the numbers on which option gives you the best CAC.

— [YOUR NAME]
warm_03 · referral_request when: a project just wrapped and went well
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Loved working on [PROJECT] with you. I'm opening two creator slots for [NEXT MONTH] and wanted to ask: is there a brand in your network you think I should know? Ideally [YOUR IDEAL CUSTOMER: e.g. early-stage DTC, beauty/wellness, active on TikTok].

If yes, a one-sentence intro is all I need. And if you wouldn't mind dropping a quick testimonial about our project — even a few lines — I'll send you a $50 gift card to say thank you.

— [YOUR NAME]

// scripts_03 · follow_up

Follow-up scripts.

The 3-touch sequence for leads that went quiet after the initial pitch. Most deals close on touch 2 or 3, not touch 1.

follow_up_01 · day_4 touch 2 · short & low-pressure
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Floating this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried. No pressure — just wanted to make sure you saw the concept.

If it's a "not right now", totally fine, just let me know and I'll stop bugging you.

— [YOUR NAME]
follow_up_02 · day_10 touch 3 · add new value
Hi [FIRST NAME],

One more from me. Since I sent the original pitch, I actually shot a spec concept for [BRAND] — you can see it here: [LOOM / DRIVE LINK].

It's 40 seconds. If you love it, let's talk. If not, I'll go quiet.

— [YOUR NAME]
follow_up_03 · day_21 · the_breakup touch 4 · final · works ~30% of the time
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Since I haven't heard back, I'm going to assume the timing's not right and close the loop on my end. Totally understand.

If things shift, or if you'd rather I check back in [Q2 / next quarter / after launch], just reply "later" and I'll queue it for then.

Either way, big fan of what [BRAND] is doing. Go build cool stuff.

— [YOUR NAME]

// scripts_04 · recovery

Recovery scripts.

When a deal goes sideways — missed deadline, bad review, ghosted brand. These scripts bring the project back without sounding defensive.

recovery_01 · missed_deadline when: you're behind because of something on your side
Hey [FIRST NAME],

Straight talk — I'm not going to hit the [DATE] delivery. [1-LINE REAL REASON, NO EXCUSES]. The new delivery date will be [NEW DATE], and I'm giving you a 10% discount on this invoice as a gesture for the delay.

Here's what I've done to make sure it doesn't happen again: [ONE CONCRETE CHANGE].

If this doesn't work for your timeline, let me know and we can walk through options.

— [YOUR NAME]
recovery_02 · critical_feedback when: the first cut wasn't what they wanted
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thanks for the honest feedback on Round 1. I hear you on [THE SPECIFIC POINT] — that's on me for not catching it in the brief.

Here's my plan for Round 2:
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 1]
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 2]
→ [SPECIFIC CHANGE 3]

I'll have revised cuts to you by [DATE]. Before I start, quick gut-check: does this direction match what you had in mind, or do you want me to send a new concept board first?

— [YOUR NAME]
recovery_03 · ghosted_mid_project when: the brand stopped replying after concepts
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Haven't heard back on the concept board I sent on [DATE]. I know launches get chaotic — so a quick call:

→ If you want to move ahead, I need the concept approval by [DATE + 3 DAYS] to hit the original delivery date.
→ If priorities have shifted, reply "pause" and I'll hold the deposit and pick this back up whenever you're ready (within 90 days).
→ If it's a hard no, reply "cancel" and we'll close it out per the SOW terms.

No wrong answer — just need a direction.

— [YOUR NAME]
